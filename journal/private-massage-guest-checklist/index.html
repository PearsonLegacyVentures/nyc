Know what to prepare for a hotel, residence or villa massage enquiry in New York City.

Prepare a suitable location

A comfortable, private and safe room matters more than decorative setup. Confirm access instructions, ventilation, floor space and any stairs or gates.

Give realistic timing

In NYC, buildings, boroughs and therapist licensing come before assuming any instant appointment. Please allow at least six hours for an enquiry, and longer when travel or staffing requires it.

Ask for the full financial picture

A quoted amount should clearly cover the treatment, guest count, duration and any agreed travel or venue costs before payment. Sending a WhatsApp or email request is not a confirmed appointment.

What happens next?

Select your desired treatment, duration and exact property. A six-hour minimum notice applies, with longer advance notice possibly required. We will verify the appropriate local practitioner, all applicable access conditions and the full price before any appointment is confirmed.
